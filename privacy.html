Browser / App LocalStorage: The app uses device-local localStorage strictly to save your preferences and site audit data on your own device—specifically: your selected language, dial theme, 8/16/32 zone mode, manual calibration offset, disclaimer acceptance record (kuberan_shastra_disclaimer_accepted), and your saved property room audit entries (kuberan_site_walkthrough_logs). No personal data, walkthrough logs, location coordinates, or custom device identifiers are transmitted; only standard HTTPS transport metadata (such as your IP address) is processed by GitHub Pages to serve the static file.

Local Data Retention: Because all preferences and kuberan_site_walkthrough_logs reside exclusively on your device, they are retained locally only until you erase them using the button below, clear app storage in OS settings, or uninstall the app. Kuberan Silks retains zero cloud copies.
